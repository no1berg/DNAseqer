Project Background

DNAseqer was created to provide a fast and accessible way to analyze DNA sequences without the need for complex software installations or dependencies. It is written in Python and designed to run on any system with minimal setup.

Goals

  • Provide a lightweight tool for basic DNA sequence analysis.
  • Enable quick transcription and translation of DNA to RNA and Protein sequences.
  • Support identification of open reading frames (ORFs) and GC content analysis.
  • Maintain simplicity and ease of use through a command-line interface.

Development Status

DNAseqer is currently a work in progress. While the core functionality is implemented, additional features and improvments are planned. Contributions and feedback are welcome.
