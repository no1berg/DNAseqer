Why DNAseqer?

DNAseqer is designed for quick, simple DNA analysis without heavy dependencies.

Features

  • Transcribes DNA to RNA
  • Translates RNA to protein
  • Finds open reading frames (ORFs)
  • Calculates GC content
  • Lightweight and easy to use
